[Adblock Plus 2.0]
! Title: Stronguard Video
! Description: Video/audio ads on any site. Ad SDKs are swapped for stand-ins that
!   report "no ad", and ad requests get an empty ad schedule, so players skip the
!   ad break cleanly instead of hanging. The extension's player ad guard and HLS
!   pruning handle what still gets through.
! Expires: 7 days
!
! --- Google IMA SDK -> stand-in that answers every ad request with "no ad" ---
||imasdk.googleapis.com/js/sdkloader/ima3.js$script,redirect=google-ima.js
||imasdk.googleapis.com/js/sdkloader/ima3_debug.js$script,redirect=google-ima.js
!

||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ads?*output=xml_vast$xmlhttprequest,redirect=noopvast-4.0
||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ads?*output=vmap$xmlhttprequest,redirect=noopvmap-1.0
||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/ads?*output=xml_vmap$xmlhttprequest,redirect=noopvmap-1.0
||pubads.g.doubleclick.net/gampad/live/ads?$xmlhttprequest,redirect=noopvast-4.0
||search.spotxchange.com/vast/$xmlhttprequest,redirect=noopvast-4.0
||search.spotxchange.com/vmap/$xmlhttprequest,redirect=noopvmap-1.0
||fwmrm.net/ad/g/1?*resp=vast$xmlhttprequest,redirect=noopvast-4.0
